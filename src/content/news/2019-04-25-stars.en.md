This evening, the Department of Psychology held its 2019 Student Thesis and Research Symposium (STARS) event. Outstanding undergraduates from a variety of labs presented their latest research in five-minute flash talks, followed by a poster session for all attendees.

The Austerweil Lab is once again proud to have Jacqueline Erens present her latest research, which explores how differences in the amount of information carried by synonyms affect word choice.
