After nine wonderful years at the University of Wisconsin–Madison, Joe moved to the [Chiba Institute of Technology](https://chibatech.jp/english/) in June 2025. He serves on the faculty as a co-founding member of the newly established [Chiba Tech School of Design & Science](https://sds.chibatech.dev). Until the school opens in April 2026, he is working at the [Chiba Tech Henkaku Center](https://www.henkaku.center/en). Admissions for the inaugural cohort are currently underway. For information on how to apply to the school, please see the [Chiba Tech School of Design & Science application page](https://sds.chibatech.dev/apply). The application deadline for the inaugural cohort is July 14, 2025.

He plans to establish a new research lab here, the Computational Mind Laboratory. More details will follow. If you have any questions or inquiries, please feel free to get in touch.
