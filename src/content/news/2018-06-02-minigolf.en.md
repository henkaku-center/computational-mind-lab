To celebrate the end of another successful semester, the Austerweil Lab held a mini golf party at Vitense Golfland. Many of us picked up a golf club for the first time that day (this author included), and most of our balls rolled everywhere except into the hole, which made for an evening full of laughter.

[![alt text](/news/assets/2018-minigolf/minigolf_group.jpg)
*From left: Xiayuan, Jeff, Jonathan (partly hidden behind Shrek), Steven, Joe, Sean, and Xian. Photo courtesy of Alyssa*](/news/assets/2018-minigolf/minigolf_group.jpg)

The event was bittersweet, since Sean Chang, a valued member of the lab since 2017, will soon be leaving. Everyone in the lab wishes him all the best in what comes next, and we would be very lucky to have him back someday.

At the end of the night, the scores were tallied (I believe Jeff won?), tears were shed (for Sean, of course), and everyone had a great time together. We are already looking forward to the next party.
