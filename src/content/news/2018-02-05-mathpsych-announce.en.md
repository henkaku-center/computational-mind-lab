Professor Austerweil is serving as a co-organizer of Math Psych 2018, which will be held at UW-Madison from July 21 to 24.

Are you interested in the mind and brain? In AI, machine learning, HCI, or computational modeling? In combining them? Please submit an abstract! The abstract deadline is March 15, 2018.

We will share more details as they become available. For now, please see the official [website](http://mathpsych.org/conferences/2018/) for more information.
