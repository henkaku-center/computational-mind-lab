2019 is shaping up to be an exciting year for the Austerweil Lab. In particular, both our staff and our student membership have grown substantially.

Since late 2018, we have had the honor of welcoming Mohsen Afrasiabi as our new postdoctoral researcher. His technical expertise is arguably unmatched. Some say his sense of humor is *definitely* unmatched as well.

We are delighted to welcome three motivated graduate students: Pablo Andre Caceres, Ian Johnston, and Michael Payton. We are also honored to have Sam Westby joining us as an undergraduate researcher.

In addition, Sean Chang will be returning to the lab as an official member within the next few weeks. We warmly welcome him back.

The Austerweil Lab officially welcomes all of our new members. We look forward to an exciting research season! For more details, please see the updated [member list](/en/people/).
