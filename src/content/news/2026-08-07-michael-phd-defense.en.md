Congratulations to **Dr. Michael Payton**, who passed his dissertation defense on
*A Computational Framework for Perseverative Thought*.

Michael's dissertation grew out of the lab's
[Templeton-funded work on rumination](/en/news/2023-07-31-templeton-rumination/),
which starts from the question of what problem repetitive negative thinking might
be *solving* rather than assuming it is simply a malfunction.

We are looking forward to writing the work up as a journal publication over the
next few months.
