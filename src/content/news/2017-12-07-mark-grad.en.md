Congratulations to Mark Ho, who has successfully defended his dissertation and earned his PhD in Cognitive Science.

Mark's next adventure is a new chapter as a postdoctoral researcher at UC Berkeley, working with Tom Griffiths and Sanjit Seshia. To send him off, the Austerweil Lab gave him a companion to accompany him to the West Coast.

The two seem to be getting along very well. We wish him all the best for the future!
