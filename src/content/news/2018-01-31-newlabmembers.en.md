Shi Xian Liew（シーシェン・リュー）and Nicole Beckage（ニコール・ベッケージ）joined the lab in January 2018.

Shi Xian Liew has just completed a PhD in psychology at the University of Melbourne, where they worked with Dr. Daniel Little（ダニエル・リトル）. Their expertise is in computational models of categorization and decision making. They are starting with research on how people generate new categories.

Nicole Beckage holds a PhD in computer science from the University of Colorado Boulder. Working with Professor Austerweil, they are applying network-based methods, including multiplex networks, to the study of cognition. They are also part of the DARPA I2O computational social simulation team, where they build agent-based models of large, interacting networks in order to understand human social interaction and communication.

Everyone in the Austerweil Lab warmly welcomes both of you to Madison!
