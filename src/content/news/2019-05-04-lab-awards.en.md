Riding the excitement of [our previous post](/en/news/2019-05-01-new-members/), 2019 is shaping up to be another fruitful year for the lab. Our members have already won a remarkable number of awards. The year is not even half over, and four members have already received formal recognition for their outstanding accomplishments.

- Jacqueline Erens and Van Rao received Hilldale Summer Research Fellowships
- Sam Westby received a Summer Honors Senior Thesis Award
- Joe Austerweil received a Distinguished Honors Faculty Award

Congratulations to all of the award winners on their outstanding achievements!
