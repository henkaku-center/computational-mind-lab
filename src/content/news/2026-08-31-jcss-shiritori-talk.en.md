Joe presents [Rule-ambiguity and distributional play in asynchronous L2
shiritori](/papers/files/AusterweilNishikawaMorita2026Shiritori.pdf) in the
International Session of the 43rd Annual Meeting of the Japanese Cognitive
Science Society, joint work with [Jumpei
Nishikawa](https://researchmap.jp/j_nishikawa?lang=en) (Okayama Prefectural
University) and [Junya Morita](https://www.inf.shizuoka.ac.jp/en/teacher/morita_junya/)
(Shizuoka University).

[Talk slides](/news/assets/2026-08-31-jcss-shiritori-talk/shiritori-jcss2026-talk.pdf) ·
[4-page paper](/papers/files/AusterweilNishikawaMorita2026Shiritori.pdf)

More on the [paper itself](/en/news/2026-03-20-jcss-shiritori/), including
what 9,891 moves of a Japanese word-chain game reveal about how
second-language learners retrieve words under a phonological constraint.
